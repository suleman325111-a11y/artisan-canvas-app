# Artisan Canvas — Fine Art Gallery & Customization Studio

A modern, vibrant, and interactive art e-commerce web application featuring a real-time **3D Room Customizer Studio**, curated fine art gallery, dynamic price calculator, customer custom photo uploader, bespoke commission inquiry workflow, and persistent shopping cart with order checkout simulation.

---

## 🎨 Key Features

### 1. Interactive 3D Room Customizer & Visualizer
- **Live Room Wall Previews**: Switch seamlessly between **Designer Living Room**, **Minimalist Master Suite**, **Executive Creative Studio**, and **White Cube Gallery Wall**.
- **Proportional Wall Scaling**: Dynamic scaling engine calibrated to standard interior wall proportions:
  - *Studio Petite*: 16" × 20" (40 × 50 cm)
  - *Gallery Classic*: 24" × 36" (60 × 90 cm) *(Most Popular)*
  - *Statement Grand*: 32" × 48" (80 × 120 cm)
  - *Architectural Oversized*: 40" × 60" (100 × 150 cm)
- **Handcrafted Solid Hardwood Frames**:
  - *Florentine Brushed Gold*
  - *Natural Scandinavian Oak*
  - *Minimalist Matte Obsidian*
  - *Nordic Gallery White*
  - *Heritage Ornate Walnut*
  - *Frameless Gallery Wrap*
- **Museum Acid-Free Matting**: None, 2-Inch Museum Ivory Linen, or 2-Inch Contrast Charcoal Mat.
- **Canvas Textures & Finishes**:
  - *Stretched Cotton Canvas* (Archival matte varnish)
  - *Textured Heavy Impasto* (Master studio hand-embellished relief brushstrokes)
  - *Museum Rag Fine Art Paper* (German etching watercolor feel)
  - *Vibrant Luminescent Gloss/Metal* (Dye-sublimation gloss depth)
- **Lighting Ambience Simulation**:
  - Natural Daylight
  - Gallery Spotlight
  - Warm Evening Glow
- **Upload Your Own Photo/Artwork**: Customers can upload personal photos or designs to test them live in any frame, room interior, and canvas material.

### 2. Curated Fine Art Gallery
- Filter by category: **Abstract**, **Landscape**, **Botanical**, **Modern Surrealism**, **Impressionist**.
- Real-time search across titles, artists, and mediums.
- Sorting by Featured, Price (Low to High), Price (High to Low), and Highest Rated.
- Color palette badges and Quick View details modal.
- One-click transfer from any gallery artwork straight into the 3D Customizer.

### 3. Dynamic Price Engine & E-Commerce Cart
- Real-time price breakdown updating as sizes, materials, frames, and mats change.
- Sliding cart drawer with custom configuration breakdown.
- Quantity adjustment, item removal, and persistent `localStorage` storage.
- Free Insured White-Glove Delivery progress bar (threshold: $250).
- Promo coupon system:
  - Enter **`ARTLOVE15`** for 15% off.
  - Enter **`GALLERYVIP`** for 20% off.
- Complete Checkout Modal & instant printable invoice order confirmation.

### 4. Bespoke Custom Commission Studio
- Direct inquiry form for customers to request custom oil/acrylic paintings from scratch by resident gallery artists.
